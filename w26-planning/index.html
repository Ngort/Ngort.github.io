03 / THE DAYS YOU NEED

Holiday, remote or leave?

Dec 24–31 is the example closure, inclusive. Jan 1 remains a workday unless confirmed above. Workdays use Boston time, Monday–Friday. A travel overlap suggests leave/flex; a day outside the closure without overlap is a remote candidate, subject to approval. Buffers include ground travel. Remote work on a flight is not assumed.

04 / RABBIT DUTY

Care for the whole absence.

Boarding: published $30/day, availability unknown. Two daily visits: $60/day + $20/holiday-rated day + $15 first meeting. Friend plan: daily care plus one professional visit per started week. Care transfers are additional. Food/supplies ordinarily used at home are excluded.

Late arrivals may extend care into the next day. Buffers automatically account for this; add extra days for boarding dropoff/delays. Weekly professional visits alone cannot cover a rabbit’s daily needs. Rabbit travel to Virginia remains unverified; all quoted flight plans leave the rabbit with care at home.
