04 / CAR-FREE REALITY CHECK

What changes the plan?

  • Roanoke: no Boston nonstop on the airport’s destination list. Rail is a long travel day. Confirm the Host pickup or exact final-mile cost; airport access is not the same as reaching a host’s home.
  • London: ordinary public transport stops on Christmas Day. Stay within walking distance or price a taxi.
  • Berlin: stock up before Dec 25/26 and Jan 1 closures; plan for a noisy NYE if fireworks are unwelcome.
  • Rabbit: taking him to Virginia is unresolved. American/Delta/Allegiant cabin policies and Amtrak do not provide a rabbit option. Frontier accepts domestic rabbits but no usable Roanoke itinerary is established. Require operating-carrier confirmation, veterinary advice and onward transport before relying on this.
